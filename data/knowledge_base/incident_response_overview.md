# Incident Response Overview (Prototype Demonstration Material)

This original demonstration playbook supports a defensive prototype; it is not authoritative legal or professional advice.

Use the sequence prepare, detect, analyze, contain, eradicate, recover, and learn. Open an incident record, assign an owner, record decisions and UTC timestamps, and preserve relevant evidence. Prioritize safety and business continuity. Scope affected identities, endpoints, applications, and data before declaring containment. Escalate early when critical services, regulated data, or an active adversary may be involved.

Containment should be proportionate: isolate affected endpoints, disable or restrict compromised identities, block validated indicators, and preserve volatile evidence when feasible. After eradication, restore from trusted sources, validate security controls, monitor for recurrence, and complete a lessons-learned review.

Hungarian retrieval aliases: incidenskezelés, kiberbiztonsági incidens, észlelés, elemzés, izolálás, felszámolás, helyreállítás, tanulságok.

## Quality principles

Separate facts, hypotheses, and unknowns. Prefer reversible containment while evidence is collected. Assign explicit owners and deadlines. Reassess classification, severity, scope, and notification needs whenever new evidence arrives. A response is not complete until business recovery and security validation both succeed.
