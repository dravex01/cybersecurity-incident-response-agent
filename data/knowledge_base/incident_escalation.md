# Incident Escalation (Prototype Demonstration Material)

Escalate immediately for ransomware, active lateral movement, privileged identity compromise, critical-service impact, credible sensitive-data exposure, safety risk, or uncontrolled external access. Assign an incident commander and establish technical, business, legal/privacy, and communications workstreams appropriate to impact.

Report confirmed facts separately from hypotheses. Maintain a timeline, action log, owners, decision rationale, and next update time. Use approved out-of-band communications if normal collaboration systems may be compromised. External notification and law-enforcement decisions belong to authorized organizational leaders and counsel.

Hungarian retrieval aliases: incidens eszkaláció, incidensvezető, kritikus rendszer, vezetői értesítés, adatvédelmi felelős, jogi csapat, sürgős incidens.

Use severity changes as new facts arrive. Escalate on credible evidence, not certainty alone, when delay could increase harm. Every handoff should include confirmed facts, hypotheses, affected scope, completed containment, evidence location, open decisions, owners, and the next update time.
